# Fleet logo artwork sources

- `hyundai.svg`, `mercedes.svg`, and `toyota.svg` are the corresponding SVG marks from `car-brand-logos@1.0.0` (`vehiclespecs/brand-logos`). The collection structure is MIT-licensed; the marks remain trademarks of their respective owners.
- `iran-khodro-diesel.png` is the Iran Khodro Diesel mark shown on its Wikipedia article: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran_Khodro_Diesel
- `scania.svg`, `volvo.svg`, and `iveco.svg` use the brand paths from Simple Icons, recolored with their respective brand colors for contrast on the light logo plates.

These marks are displayed only to identify vehicle manufacturers and do not imply sponsorship or endorsement.
