faqs:
  - q: "What is a staff shuttle service?"
    a: "A staff shuttle is contracted, scheduled transport between employees' homes and their workplace. It runs with buses, minibuses, vans or cars on agreed routes and times, and differs from taxis or public transport because capacity, stops and timings are defined for one organisation."
  - q: "How is employee shuttle different from factory shift transport?"
    a: "Employee shuttle services focus on daily urban routes for office and general staff, while factory shift transport is built around production shift start and end times, often across two or three shifts. Both can be combined in a single agreement."
  - q: "How is staff shuttle pricing calculated?"
    a: "Pricing depends on distance and the number of routes, stops, shifts, vehicle count and class, and contract length. To receive an estimate, send the pick-up and destination list, the shift table and an approximate passenger count so the proposal covers the same scope."
  - q: "Which areas around Qazvin are covered?"

  - q: "Is there a minimum number of passengers?"
    a: "No fixed minimum is published. Vans are proposed for smaller groups and minibuses or buses for larger ones; passenger numbers on each route determine whether the route is economically viable."
  - q: "Can routes change after the contract starts?"
    a: "Yes. Stop or departure-time changes are reviewed in advance. It is better to define a route review mechanism in the contract so changes can be applied without interrupting the service."
  - q: "How does a staff shuttle relate to labour law?"
    a: "Under Article 152 of the Iranian Labour Code, when a workplace is outside the urban area and has no access to public transport, providing staff transport is the employer's responsibility. Organisations usually meet this through a contract with a transport provider; the exact position of each case should be reviewed with the organisation's legal adviser."
